Privacy Policy for Nokee

This policy applies to the Nokee application for Android (package com.nokee.aiprivateoffline) and to this policy page.

1. Summary

Nokee is a notes app built on one principle: your notes belong to you and stay on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no Nokee server that stores your content.

In short:

  • We do not collect any personal data. No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking identifiers, no advertising ID.
  • We do not share or sell any data, because we never receive it.
  • Notes, tags, attachments and AI chats live in the app's private storage on your device and leave it only when you actively export, share or open them.
  • The optional AI assistant runs fully offline. The one network request Nokee makes by itself is the one-time download of the AI model file from Hugging Face, which you start yourself and can decline.
  • You are in control: delete any note, clear AI chats, delete the AI model, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

In Google Play's Data safety section, Nokee is declared as collecting no data and sharing no data. This policy explains why that is accurate and what happens locally on your device.

2. Who we are and what this policy covers

“We” and “us” in this policy mean the developer of the Nokee app (“Nokee”, the data controller where that concept applies). You can reach us at the address in section 15.

This policy covers the Nokee Android application, the data it processes on your device, and the few interactions it has with services outside your device. It does not cover third-party services you choose to use yourself (for example the app store you installed Nokee from, the system speech-recognition service, or a cloud drive where you save an exported backup). Those services have their own privacy policies.

3. What data we collect, and what we do with it

We collect no personal data and no sensitive user data. Nokee has no user accounts, no backend, and no telemetry. The table below lists every category of data Play's Data safety form asks about, and what actually happens in Nokee.

Table 1 — Data categories and Nokee's behaviour.

Notes, tags and other user content No No Stored only in the app's private database and files on your device. Never transmitted to us or to any AI service.
Files and attachments you add No No You pick them through the system file picker; a copy is stored in the app's private storage.
Photos No No Only if you attach a picture to a note. Attached photos are processed on the device; with the text-only AI models Nokee ships they are not used by the AI at all.

Because we do not collect any of the above, there is no user data for us to sell, rent, profile, transfer or disclose to advertisers, data brokers or any other third party.

4. Data stored on your device

All your content is created and kept locally, inside the app's private storage:

  • Notes and tags — a local database (nokee_vault.db, Room/SQLite) in the app's private directory.
  • Attachments — files in the app's private storage. When you open an attachment in another app, Android hands that app a temporary, read-only content URI for a copy in Nokee's cache; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • AI chats and the AI model — only if you use the AI feature: the chat transcript and, if you downloaded it, the model file live in the app's private storage.
  • Preferences — theme, onboarding state and similar settings, stored locally.
  • Diagnostics — optional plain-text notes about AI start-up failures, stored locally (see section 10).

Nokee is installed with Android backups disabled (allowBackup="false"), so your notes are not copied to Google's or anyone else's cloud backup service. Backups happen only when you export one yourself, and they are written wherever you choose in the system file picker — which may be a location your own cloud provider syncs. What happens to a file after you export or share it is governed by the app or service you chose.

5. Permissions Nokee requests

Nokee asks for the smallest set of Android permissions it can work with:

Table 2 — Android permissions and their only purpose.
Permission Why it is requested
Internet Used for one thing: the optional, user-started download of the on-device AI model file. Nokee works fully without it — skip the AI download and no data ever leaves the device.
Network state To detect when an unmetered (Wi-Fi) connection is available so the AI model download can wait for it. No connection data is stored or transmitted.
Notifications To show the download progress notification while the AI model is fetching in the background. Nothing else is ever notified.
Foreground service (data sync) To keep the AI model download alive and resumable while you use other apps.

Nokee never requests, and cannot access:

  • microphone — voice input is handled by the system speech-recognition app (see section 7), not by Nokee;
  • camera, photos library or media permissions — you choose files through the system picker;
  • location, contacts, calendar, phone, SMS, health or fitness data;
  • broad storage access — Nokee only reads and writes its own private directory and grants temporary access to files you pick;
  • accessibility, device-admin, overlay or usage-stats privileges.

6. The optional on-device AI assistant

Nokee includes an optional assistant that answers questions about your notes. It is off until you download a model, and you can stay text-free of it forever without losing any notes feature.

Downloading the model

When you tap the download button in the Vault, Nokee fetches a public, open-weights model file (a Llama 3.2 quantisation, roughly 1.8 GB or 3 GB depending on your device) over HTTPS from the model host huggingface.co / its CDN. As with any web request, that host sees standard connection data such as your IP address and a generic Nokee user-agent string; it is outside our control and we never receive a copy of it. No content of your notes, prompts or chats is included in that request. The downloaded file is verified against a SHA-256 checksum pinned in the app before it is used, and you can delete it at any time.

Using the assistant

  • Everything is computed on your device. The prompt you type, the content of the note being discussed and the answer are processed locally by the model. They are not sent to us, to the model's original authors, or to any AI service or API.
  • Answers may be wrong. The assistant is a small language model running on a phone; treat its output as a suggestion, not as a fact or professional advice.
  • Nothing is applied to your notes automatically. When you ask for a rewrite, Nokee proposes specific edits that you must review and confirm.
  • Files are not read by the AI. Attachments such as PDFs, XML or JSON are never fed to the model, and with the text-only models Nokee ships, photos are not analysed either.
  • Your chats are yours. Transcripts are stored locally so the conversation has context, and they are visible and deletable in the app.

Removing it

“Delete AI data” in the Vault removes the model file and the AI chats from your device. Uninstalling Nokee removes them as well.

7. Voice input

The microphone button in the assistant opens your phone's existing speech-recognition service through the standard Android recognition intent. Nokee does not record audio, does not request the microphone permission, and never receives your voice: the recogniser app captures the audio and returns plain text, which is what you see in the input field.

Important: the speech-recognition service is provided by your device manufacturer or by Google, and depending on which one is installed and how it is configured (for example “improve speech recognition” or “offline speech recognition” settings) it may process the audio on your device or send it to that provider's servers. That processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy, not by this one. If you do not want that, simply type your question instead of using the microphone.

8. Third parties and sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or trade your data, because we do not hold it. Nokee contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no attribution SDK, no social plug-in, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking technology of any kind.

The only parties that can be involved when you use certain features are:

  • The AI model host (Hugging Face and its CDN) — only during the model download you start, and only as a file host. It receives a request for a public file, nothing about your notes.
  • The speech-recognition provider — only if you use voice input, as explained in section 7.
  • Apps you share with yourself — when you export a backup, share a note or open an attachment, Android hands the file or a temporary read-only URI to the app or destination you picked. That is always an action you take, and it is governed by the receiving app's policy.
  • The app store you installed Nokee from (for example Google Play) — stores collect their own installation, device and usage information to deliver updates, run their services and detect abuse. That is independent of Nokee and governed by the store's own privacy policy.

Nokee also has no server of its own. There is no account system, no sync service, no advertising ID usage and no data broker relationship. Nothing about you is disclosed to any government or third party by us, because there is nothing to disclose.

9. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your notes, attachments, preferences, AI chats and the AI model stay on your device for as long as you keep them there. We hold no copy, so there is no retention period on our side and nothing for us to delete on request.

You are always in control of the local data:

  • Delete a note, tag or attachment — from the note list or the editor; deletions are permanent.
  • Delete AI chats — clear or delete individual conversations in the AI screen.
  • Delete the AI model — “Delete AI data” in the Vault frees the model file and the chats.
  • Remove everything at once — uninstalling Nokee deletes its private storage, including notes, attachments, chats and the model.
  • Diagnostics — the local diagnostic files are removed with the app's data as well, and can be cleared by deleting app storage.

If you believe we hold some data about you (we do not), you can contact us at the address in section 15 and we will respond within 30 days.

10. How your data is protected

  • Private app storage. Notes, attachments, chats and settings are written to Nokee's private directory, which the Android sandbox prevents other apps from reading on a normal, unrooted device.
  • No account to compromise. There is no Nokee login, no password, no token and no server holding your content, so there is no online account that could be breached or leaked.
  • Verified downloads. The AI model file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 checksum before it is ever loaded; a file that does not match is deleted and never used.
  • Local-only AI. Inference happens in the app process, with no network call involved.
  • Optional local diagnostics. If the AI engine fails to start, Nokee can write a plain-text file (for example, that the process was killed while loading the model and how much memory the device had). It stays in Nokee's private storage and is shared only when you explicitly tap the share action and pick a destination. It contains no note content.
  • Your device's own protection still matters. Local files are not additionally encrypted by Nokee, so anyone who can unlock your phone (or a device-level backup you create) can read a note, exactly as they could read any other file you keep on the device. Use a screen lock and keep your device updated.

11. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California) and similar regimes give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port personal data, and to object to its processing or sale. Nokee is designed so that these rights need no request round-trip:

  • Access and portability — all of your data is on your device and can be exported at any time (JSON backup or Markdown archive).
  • Correction — edit any note or AI chat directly in the app.
  • Deletion — delete content in the app or uninstall Nokee; there is no remote copy to erase.
  • Objection, restriction and opt-out of sale/sharing — nothing is processed remotely, nothing is sold or shared, and no advertising or profiling takes place, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • No automated decision-making with legal effect — the AI assistant only drafts text for you to accept or discard.

Where a legal basis is required for a processing operation, it is your consent (starting the AI model download, using voice input) or the performance of the service you asked for (storing your notes). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the AI data or simply not using those features.

12. Children's privacy

Nokee is a general-purpose notes app that is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and it is not designed for or marketed to children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children — in fact we do not collect personal information from anyone. Because no data is gathered, no verifiable parental consent process is required. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has somehow provided us with personal data, contact us and we will look into it and delete anything found.

13. International users and data transfers

Nokee has no servers, so we do not transfer your content across borders. The only cross-border interactions are the ones described above: the download request to the model host's global CDN, the speech-recognition call if you use voice input, and the app store you installed from. Those providers may process connection data (such as your IP address) in countries other than yours under their own policies. Nokee itself is designed to work identically anywhere, without regional differences, and this policy page is publicly accessible worldwide without registration or geo-restrictions.
